# Alignment and Course Design Story

The Alignment workspace is a faculty reflection aid, not a course-quality score. Its primary view presents each student learning outcome as an explicit pathway:

`Outcome → Practice → Evidence`

Practice is drawn only from saved activities linked to the outcome. Evidence is drawn only from assessments linked to the outcome. The workspace labels these structural states descriptively: Complete pathway, Practice not yet connected, Evidence not yet connected, Outcome only, and Multiple connections. A complete pathway means that relationships are represented in the course record; it does not claim that the design is educationally complete or effective.

The Matrix view is a compact scan of those same relationships. “Explore this pathway” asks faculty questions about feedback, revision, evidence, and level of thinking, then routes them back to the existing Outcomes, Assessments, and Learning Experiences tools. It does not introduce a second recommendation or scoring engine.

The Course Design Story composes the existing persistent decision records. It shows the most recent saved outcome and assessment decision for each item, all approved learning-experience decisions, and the current pathways. Original and current outcome wording remain visible in the outcome decision record. The underlying records remain append-only; the story intentionally uses the latest record per outcome or assessment so its summary is readable.

The workspace’s “One possible next step” is deliberately conservative. It first identifies outcomes without evidence, then outcomes with evidence but no practice, then assessments linked to several outcomes. Otherwise it invites the faculty member to review the Design Story. These observations rely entirely on explicit saved relationships and never call AI.
