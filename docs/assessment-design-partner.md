# Assessment Design Partner milestone

Assessment Design Partner extends backward design from outcomes to evidence. Syllabus parsing remains a source of detected facts only. Faculty-confirmed `linkedSloIds` are the authoritative assessment-to-outcome relationship used by Alignment.

Curated assessment patterns—authenticity, formative checkpoints, visible thinking, transparency, student choice, peer/self assessment, AI-era redesign, and help-decide—provide short explanations, selectable moves, and editable drafts. They do not infer discipline-specific content or automatically rewrite assessments.

Approved choices are saved as `assessmentDesignDecisions` with the original snapshot, confirmed outcome links, selected moves, design draft, provenance, and timestamp. The assessment-to-practice handoff stores a lightweight `practiceTarget`, allowing Learning Activities to prepopulate the relevant outcome and assessment without duplicating the Activity Builder.
